01Finish

Two finishes, one clean line

A crisp extruded aluminium housing with a flush opal diffuser. Specified in matt black or white as standard, or any RAL to match the interior — with no visible fixings down the run.

  • 01 Matt black & white as standard
  • 02 Any RAL finish to order
  • 03 Flush opal, UGR<19, wallwash or louvre optics
02Continuous runs

Built to run line after line

Estrella links end-to-end into long, dot-free runs and turns through L-shapes and rectangular frames — so a single specified detail can carry across a whole floorplate.

  • 01 Continuous, joint-free diffusion
  • 02 L-shapes & rectangular frames
  • 03 Integral 3hr emergency & indirect up-light options
Why specify Estrella

Low-glare optics

UGR<19 microprismatic and louvre options for screen-based and workplace environments.

CRI 90 light quality

High colour rendering across 3000K and 4000K, calibrated so every metre of a run matches.

Made in Britain

Engineered, assembled and tested in Manchester — built to order, not to a catalogue.

5-year warranty

Long-life LED modules and drivers backed by a five-year warranty as standard.

True continuous runs

Joint-free diffusion through straight lines, L-shapes and rectangular frames.

Emergency & up-light

Integral 3hr self-test emergency and indirect up-light options built into the profile.

03In-house Manufacturing

Made to order in Manchester

Estrella isn’t imported and rebadged. It’s engineered, assembled and tested under one roof in the UK — so runs are cut to your dimensions, finishes matched to your palette, and lead times stay short.

Designing and making in-house means tighter tolerances, full traceability, and the freedom to build to your geometry — to order, not to a catalogue.
