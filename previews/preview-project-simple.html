Projects  /  Workplace

A breakout space that feels like the best seat in the building.

One photograph. One idea: warm, low pools of light over a quiet corner — made to order, fitted in a day.

SectorWorkplace · Amenity
LocationManchester, UK
ProductsCylinder pendants
FinishMatte black
Completed2026
Lead timeUK-made, ~6 days

The amenity corner needed to pull people away from their desks without feeling like a meeting room. We hung a cluster of matte-black cylinder pendants at staggered heights over the seating, tuned warm and dimmable, so the light gathers around the sofa rather than washing the whole floor.

The result

A single, confident gesture. No false ceiling, no clutter — just direct, glare-controlled light that makes the space feel intentional. Proof that a project page doesn't need a gallery to land: one strong image and the right words do the work.

"They took one corner and made it the most photographed spot in the office."

— Workplace Lead
