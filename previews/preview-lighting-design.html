01How it works

Four steps from drawing to delivered scheme

01

Send your drawings

A floor plan, reflected ceiling plan or even a sketch and a finishes schedule. PDF, DWG or photos — whatever you have.

02

We design the light

Our team produces a DIALux photometric study — lux levels, uniformity, UGR and energy — matched to your finishes and budget.

03

Costed scheme in 48h

You receive a fully specified luminaire schedule, layout and itemised costing, with value-engineered alternatives where it helps.

Made & delivered
