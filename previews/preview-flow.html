03In-house Manufacturing

Home to the UK’s first automated light-bending machine

Flow+ isn’t imported and rebadged. It’s designed, bent, assembled and tested under one roof in Manchester — on the UK’s first automated light-bending machine, so every curve is repeatable to the millimetre rather than hand-formed or outsourced.

Designing and making in-house means tighter tolerances, shorter lead times, full traceability, and the freedom to build Flow+ to your geometry — to order, not to a catalogue.
