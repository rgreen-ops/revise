01 Flexibility

Shape light to the space

Flow+ bends both horizontally and vertically, tracing any architectural line without breaks, hotspots or visible joints.

  • Horizontal & vertical bending to 360°
  • Continuous, dot-free diffusion
  • Surface, suspended or recessed
02 Light Quality

Calibrated for interiors

High-CRI light with tunable white, factory-calibrated so every metre matches — across a single run or an entire scheme.

  • CRI 90+ as standard
  • 2700K – 6500K tunable white
  • DALI & phase dimming
03 Made in Britain

Engineered & built in Manchester

Flow+ isn’t imported and rebadged. It’s designed, assembled, tested and finished in our own UK facility — so we control quality, lead times and every bespoke detail.

Vertical integration means shorter lead times, full traceability and the ability to make to order rather than to a catalogue.

04 Customisation

Made to order, not off the shelf

Because we manufacture in-house, almost everything about Flow+ can be tailored to your scheme — at project quantities, without bespoke-tooling minimums.

/ LENGTH

Bespoke runs

Cut and joined to your exact dimensions — single continuous lengths or repeating modules.

/ FINISH

Any RAL finish

Powder-coated housings in standard white, black or any RAL to match the interior palette.

/ OPTIC

Custom diffusers

Opal, frosted or micro-prismatic diffusers and beam shaping for glare control.

/ COLOUR

Tuned CCT

Fixed or tunable white calibrated to a specified colour temperature and CRI.

/ MOUNTING

Mounting & trims

Surface, suspended, recessed and plaster-in trims, with custom suspension drops.

/ CONTROLS

Controls & drivers

DALI, DALI-2, phase or 1–10V, pre-wired and zoned to your control layout.
