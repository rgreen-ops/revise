Flow+ bends both horizontally and vertically, tracing any architectural line without breaks, hotspots or visible joints.

  • 02 Continuous, dot-free diffusion
  • 03 Surface, suspended or recessed

04Customisation

Made to order,
not off the shelf

Because we manufacture in-house, almost everything about Flow+ can be tailored to your scheme — at project quantities, without bespoke-tooling minimums.

01

Bespoke runs

Cut and joined to your exact dimensions — single continuous lengths or repeating modules.

02

Any RAL finish

Powder-coated housings in white, black or any RAL to match the interior palette.

03

Custom diffusers & optics

Opal, frosted or micro-prismatic diffusers and beam shaping for glare control.

04

Tuned colour

Fixed or tunable white calibrated to a specified colour temperature and CRI.

05

Mounting & trims

Surface, suspended, recessed and plaster-in trims, with custom suspension drops.

06

Controls & drivers

DALI, DALI-2, phase or 1–10V, pre-wired and zoned to your control layout.
