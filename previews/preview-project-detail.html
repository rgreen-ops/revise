Case Study · Workplace

An acoustic linear ceiling that calms a busy floor.

Sector  Commercial OfficeLocation  Manchester, UKCompleted  2026
ClientFinancial services HQ
ScopeAmenity & circulation floor
ProductsAstrawave · Flow+ · Downlights
Luminaires320 fittings
Lead designerRicoman in-house

The brief was simple to say and hard to do: make a high-traffic corridor feel quiet. Acoustics, light and rhythm had to work as one ceiling — not three trades fighting for the same plane.

The challenge

The connecting corridor between work and amenity zones was long, hard-surfaced and loud. The client wanted a calmer transition, low glare for people moving between screens and daylight, and a ceiling that felt intentional rather than service-led.

What we specified

We set Astrawave linear runs into a black perforated acoustic raft, staggering the lengths to break up the length of the corridor and soften the sound. Flow+ continues the line into the amenity space, and low-glare downlights pick up the seating pockets.

Every fitting was made to order in Manchester to the exact ceiling geometry, then delivered phased so the floor could stay in use through the fit-out.
