Case Study · Workplace

Allianz HQ — a workplace that works after dark.

SectorCommercial Office
LocationLeeds, UK
FittingsFlow+ · Edge 35 · Void 95
CompletedMarch 2026

The brief

A 6-floor headquarters refit needed a single lighting language across open-plan desks, breakout zones and a double-height reception — low glare for screens, but warmth for the social spaces.

What we specified

Continuous Flow+ runs trace the architecture through reception and circulation, while recessed Edge 35 and low-glare Void 95 downlights hold UGR<19 across every desk. All on DALI, tuned to a daily curve.

Everything was made to order in Manchester and delivered to a phased programme, floor by floor, so the client never lost a working day.
