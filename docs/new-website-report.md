# The New Ricoman Website — What's Changed and Why It Matters

*A plain-English summary for the leadership report. No technical knowledge needed.*

---

## In one paragraph

We haven't just given ricoman.com a fresh coat of paint. We've rebuilt it on
modern foundations that are **faster for customers, cheaper and more reliable to
run, and far easier for the team to keep up to date** — while quietly fixing
years of hidden technical problems that had been building up behind the scenes.
The new site also does real commercial work: it **captures sales leads,
builds its own SEO**, and lets specifiers find the right product faster.

---

## 1. We fixed years of hidden technical debt

The old website had serious problems that weren't visible from the front end but
were costing us money and putting the business at risk:

- **75,911 images in the media library — and 69,315 of them (91%) were exact
  duplicates.** These were created automatically by the old shop system, which
  added a fresh copy of the same picture for every product variant.
- **That bloat broke our hosting backups.** The site's scheduled backups had been
  **failing since around October 2025** and had ballooned to **248 GB** — meaning
  that for months, if anything had gone wrong, we may not have had a clean,
  recent backup to restore from.

The new site includes a built-in **Media Cleanup** tool that removed the
duplicates safely. The result:

- A dramatically smaller, cleaner media library.
- **Backups that are reliable again** — smaller, faster, and actually completing.
- **Lower, more predictable hosting costs** because we're storing a fraction of
  the data.

> **Headline for the report:** the new website doesn't just look better — it is
> *faster, cheaper to host, and more reliable*, and it repaired a backup failure
> that had gone unnoticed for months.

---

## 2. The team can run it themselves — no developer needed

Previously, even small changes meant going back to a developer. The new site is
built so the marketing and sales team are in control:

- **Self-serve product pages.** A custom Product Page Builder lets the team edit
  the specification and variant tables (including choosing *which columns show*)
  and drop content blocks into any product page — no code, no developer.
- **Editable landing pages.** Our feature pages (Casambi, Human Centric Lighting,
  Fire Safety, Made in Britain, Sustainability, Trade, and more) are all built
  from editable building blocks.
- **One-click content tools** in the admin area for SEO copy, page layouts and
  more, all designed so they *fill in starter content but never overwrite
  anything the team has written.*

---

## 3. It markets itself — built-in SEO

Search engine visibility is built into the site rather than bolted on:

- **Automatic internal link-building.** News articles and project case studies
  automatically link relevant terms (e.g. "linear lighting", "emergency lighting")
  to the right product, category and sector pages. This builds the internal links
  Google rewards — and guides customers towards buying — completely automatically.
- **Automatic tagging and sector links.** News auto-tags by topic; projects link
  to their sector hubs.
- **Spec filtering for specifiers.** Category pages let customers filter products
  by light output (lumens) and power (wattage), pulled from the real product data
  — so specifiers find the right fitting faster.
- **Keyword-rich category and page copy.** Every product category and every key
  page can have its own hand-written SEO title, description and intro/FAQ copy,
  turning them into proper landing pages (e.g. "Linear Lighting", "Casambi").
- **Rich Google results.** The site automatically provides the structured data
  Google uses for enhanced listings (business details, product info, breadcrumbs,
  FAQs that can appear directly in search results).
- **A built-in SEO health check.** The team can see, at a glance, which pages need
  attention — average score, and a prioritised list of anything missing.

---

## 4. It captures sales leads

The new site is an active lead-generation tool, not just a brochure:

- **Download gate.** Visitors downloading datasheets, guides or other documents
  are asked for their name, email and customer type — turning anonymous traffic
  into named, contactable leads (captured once, then remembered).
- **BIM / Revit requests, newsletter sign-ups, and "request a callback"** forms
  all feed the same system.
- **Every lead lands in one place** — a leads dashboard in the admin area (showing
  type, contact, source and date) and can sync to a spreadsheet/CRM.
- **Saved projects.** Logged-in customers can build and save named project lists
  and download a complete "project pack" (datasheets, instructions and
  photometric files, zipped up per product) in one click.
- **Spam-protected throughout**, so the team's inbox stays clean.

---

## 5. It's faster — and built to stay fast

The site is rendered natively and efficiently (no heavy page-builder overhead),
images are served in modern, lightweight formats, and only the code each page
needs is loaded. The result is a quicker experience for customers and better
search rankings (Google factors in page speed).
